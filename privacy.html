Privacy policy

Version 5 · Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the Refi Compare app and this website handle information. We choose to follow the Australian Privacy Principles. Refi Compare is made by Abbas Patel ("we", "us"). Questions: abspatel1@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We never see your loan details. The app works out your savings on your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts, no selling or sharing of data.
  • We only get personal information if you choose to email us.

Here is what the app itself says, word for word:

Your privacy

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.

Everything you type stays on this phone. We work out your savings on the phone itself, so your loan details are never sent to us, to lenders or to anyone else.

Your answers are only kept if you turn on "Remember my answers". That includes any money details you add, like pay, debts, savings and rent, and the price and deposit of a home you plan to buy. It also includes the homes and loans you add to My properties. If you turn on rate watch, it also keeps which rates you last looked at and your biggest saving then. They are then locked in your phone's secure storage for this phone only, and they don't move to a new phone through backups. Turn it off, or use "Forget my saved answers" below, and they are deleted at once.

The message for your bank is shared only if you tap Share, and only to the app you choose.

If you fill in from a photo, the words are read on this phone and the app deletes its copy of the photo straight after. The photo is never sent anywhere. The camera is used only when you tap "Take a photo".

On iPhone, deleting the app can leave your saved answers locked away on the phone. They are deleted if the app is installed again. To remove them straight away, turn the option off before deleting the app.

What the app downloads

From time to time the app downloads a file of the latest lender rates. That request contains nothing about you or your loan.

Like any website, the service that hosts the file sees your internet address so it can reply. It may keep it in its logs for a short time. We don't use it to work out who you are, and we don't combine it with anything else.

Each rates file carries our digital signature. The app checks it before using the file, so it can tell if anyone else has changed it.

If you email us

The app never sends feedback by itself. If you choose to email us, we get your email address and what you write. The message also shows the app version, your phone type and system version, and which rates you had, so we can find the problem. We use it only to reply and to fix the app. We delete it within 12 months.

Who else is involved

We use three services. Each may store information outside Australia, mainly in the United States:

  • Cloudflare stores and serves the rates file the app downloads. Its servers see the internet address of each download and may keep it in short-term logs. We do not use these logs to identify anyone.
  • Google (Gmail) holds our support mailbox, so it stores any email you send us.
  • GitHub hosts this website. It sees the internet address of visitors for security and may keep it in its logs. We get no visitor data from it. GitHub also holds our private list of things to fix, which never includes your name, email address or loan details.

We do not otherwise disclose personal information to anyone, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep things

Feedback emails are deleted within 12 months. We copy only the technical details of a problem (never your name, email address or loan details) into our private list of things to fix.

Your choices and rights

"Delete my data" (under More in the app) deletes your saved answers and money details from your phone in one step. On Android, deleting the app also deletes them. On iPhone, see the note above.

You can use every part of the app without giving us any personal information. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold by emailing abspatel1@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

Complaints

Please email us first and we will try to fix it within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Children

The app is meant for adults with a home loan. We don't knowingly receive information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the app changes. A change that means we collect something new would come with an app update that says so on its About and privacy screen.

Refi Compare has no ads, no tracking and no accounts. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
