Help and support

Refi Compare shows whether switching your home loan to another lender could save you money, using the rates Australian lenders publish. It's free, with no ads, no tracking and no sign-up.

Contact us

Email abspatel1@gmail.com. In the app, go to About and privacy and tap one of the Help and feedback buttons: your email app opens with the app version filled in. Please don't send your loan details.

Where the rates come from

Lenders publish their home loan rates and fees under the Australian Government's Consumer Data Right. About twice a week we collect what they publish, check it, and send it to the app.

We include loans for a home you live in, both variable and fixed (6 months to 10 years). Not every lender or loan is included, and special offers or discounts aren't applied.

No lender pays us, and no lender can change the order of results.

Why might my offer be different?

These are lenders' advertised rates. The rate you're offered may be different, depending on your situation. Lenders also check your income, your property and your credit history before they make an offer.

A rate looks wrong

We show what each lender publishes. If it looks different from the lender's website, please tell us the lender and loan name, and we'll check.

How do I delete my saved answers?

Open About and privacy and tap Forget my saved answers, or set "Remember my answers" to No. They are deleted from the phone at once.

Important information

This is general information, not financial advice. It doesn't consider your personal situation. Check the details with the lender before you decide.

Refi Compare has no ads, no tracking and no accounts. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
